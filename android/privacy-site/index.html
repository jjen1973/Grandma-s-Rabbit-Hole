Grandma's Rabbit Hole Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Grandma's Rabbit Hole is a children's learning and entertainment app created by Jennifer Lamonatgne. The app includes interactive stories, coloring activities, games, and other child-friendly activities.

We take children's privacy seriously and aim to collect only the information necessary for the app to work.

Information We Collect

Grandma's Rabbit Hole may allow a parent or caregiver to create a child profile using a first name or nickname so the app can personalize the child's experience.

We do not ask children to provide an email address, phone number, home address, precise location, or other unnecessary personal information.

The app may use technical information needed for normal app operation, such as information required to save progress, purchases, unlocked content, or app settings.

Firebase and Cloud Storage

Grandma's Rabbit Hole uses Google Firebase services to support app features such as saving app information, profiles, progress, unlocked content, or other app data.

Information handled through Firebase is used only to provide and maintain Grandma's Rabbit Hole and its features.

We do not sell children's personal information.

Purchases

Grandma's Rabbit Hole may offer optional digital purchases, such as unlocking additional books or activities.

Purchases made through the Android version of the app are processed by Google Play. Grandma's Rabbit Hole does not receive or store the purchaser's full credit-card or payment-card information.

Advertising

Grandma's Rabbit Hole does not display third-party advertising.

The app may occasionally show information about other Grandma's Rabbit Hole or Waffles & Pip content in a parent-facing area. This content is not third-party behavioral advertising.

Children's Privacy

Grandma's Rabbit Hole is designed for young children and their families.

We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is necessary to provide the app's features.

We do not use children's information for targeted advertising.

We do not sell or rent children's personal information.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the app, such as Google Firebase and Google Play. These services are used only for app functionality, storage, security, purchases, and related technical services.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by Grandma's Rabbit Hole. Data transmitted between the app and supported online services is sent using secure connections where supported.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app's features and maintain the user's saved information.

A parent or caregiver may request deletion of personal information associated with their child or app account by contacting us using the privacy contact below.

When a valid deletion request is received, applicable personal information will be permanently deleted unless it must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record requirements.

Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your Grandma’s Rabbit Hole account and associated data, email jenparker98@yahoo.com and include the email address used for the parent account.

External Services and Links

Grandma's Rabbit Hole may contain parent-directed links to outside services or websites. Once a user leaves Grandma's Rabbit Hole, the privacy practices of the outside service apply.

Parents and caregivers should review the privacy policies of those services separately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Grandma's Rabbit Hole gains new features. The updated policy will show a new “Last updated” date.
